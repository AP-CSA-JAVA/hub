Teacher Hub

Everything for class, quizzes and grading in one place. Each tool opens in its own tab.

In class

🎯
Roll Call
Random student caller with question banks, think timers, attendance and a scoreboard.
🏁
Roll Call Teams: Race Day
Team race: answer right to gain ground, wrong to fall back. Uses your Roll Call question banks.

Quizzes & tests

✅
Check for Understanding
Section quizzes on the course site. Results, Gate times, Reports and the Canvas export live in the quiz Sheet.
Quiz Sheet Course site
📝
40-Question Final (MCQ Exam)
Timed exam page. Sign in as the teacher to open or close it, set minutes, see results and download Canvas and PDF files.
⌨️
FRQ Writer
Students write FRQs with a keystroke log. Sign in as the teacher to open FRQs, set timers and replay sessions.
